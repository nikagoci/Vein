I created a specification for GymSplit before implementing the Python application. The specification defined the goal, acceptance criteria, constraints, out-of-scope features, verification method, and first implementation slice. I delegated the initial Python implementation to AI based on the requirements in spec.md. The implementation accepts the user's goal, experience level, training days, and available equipment, generates the workout split, enforces equipment restrictions, and rejects invalid inputs. I verified it against the three golden questions: a 4-day beginner muscle-gain plan, a 3-day intermediate fat-loss plan with limited equipment, and invalid training-day input.
